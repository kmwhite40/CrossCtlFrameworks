How the match is made

Concord keeps no separate scan-finding table: an uploaded scan is reconciled into POA&Ms, and each finding's CVE references are written into its POA&M's weakness text. This page reads CVE ids (CVE-YYYY-NNNN…) out of the title and weakness of every open or in-progress POA&M on your live systems, scan-sourced or not, and looks each one up in the catalog. It is a text match: a POA&M that describes a KEV vulnerability without naming its CVE id is not found.
