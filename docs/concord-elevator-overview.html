Concord

The self-hostable, OSCAL-native compliance engine for CUI, defense-industrial, and federal systems.

FedRAMP CMMC L2 NIST 800-53A / 800-171 OSCAL 1.1 26 Frameworks Air-Gap Capable
Compliance teams in the defense and federal space are forced to choose between cloud-only GRC tools they cannot put CUI into, and spreadsheets that do not scale. Concord is the middle path — a compliance controls platform you run inside your own security boundary. It computes a live, transparent SPRS score, generates SSPs, SARs, and OSCAL 1.1 packages, and maps a single control effort across 26 frameworks, so you author once and comply many. No per-seat SaaS tax, and no data leaving your perimeter.

Capabilities

What Concord delivers

Live CMMC L2 / SPRS scoring

A deterministic SPRS score (−203 to 110) that recomputes the moment an implementation changes — transparent and defensible, not a black box.

Document & OSCAL generation

SSP, SAR, and compliance reports in xlsx, docx, csv, and json, plus OSCAL 1.1 component-definition and SSP export aligned to FedRAMP 20x / RFC-0024.

Guided SSP authoring

Canned, Gov-cloud-aware implementation statements with organization-defined-parameter fill-in-the-blanks — author 800-171 narratives in minutes instead of from a blank page.

Always-current catalogs

A scheduled poller watches authoritative NIST OSCAL feeds and flags exactly which controls changed — your baseline never silently drifts out of date behind a static spreadsheet.

Config-capture automation

Optional Microsoft Graph (M365 Government / GCC High) and AWS GovCloud connectors read live tenant configuration to auto-populate SSP parameters in support of the controls.

Cross-framework crosswalk

Approximately 121,000 mappings across 26 frameworks — FedRAMP, CMMC, 800-171, HIPAA, ISO 27001, SOC 2, CIS v8, NIST CSF and more — with full-text search.

Compliance operations

Systems, control implementations, evidence, assessments, and POA&M and risk registers — the full ATO workflow in one governed data model.

Tamper-evident audit

A hash-chained audit log, opt-in authentication and RBAC, and database-enforced multi-tenant isolation via PostgreSQL row-level security.

Deploy anywhere

Docker and PostgreSQL. A FastAPI service, browser UI, command-line interface, and documented REST API — installable in air-gapped environments.

The Problem

Industry needs Concord answers

CUI cannot go to the cloud

Most modern GRC tools are SaaS-only and off-limits for ITAR and classified DIB workloads. Concord runs inside your boundary.

OSCAL is becoming mandatory

FedRAMP 20x and RFC-0024 push machine-readable packages toward roughly September 2026. Concord already ships OSCAL export.

SPRS math is opaque

Most enterprise GRC does not compute SPRS at all. Concord shows a live, deterministic score you can defend to an assessor.

Map once, comply many

Redundant control work across frameworks is the leading GRC cost. Concord's crosswalk collapses it into a single effort.
